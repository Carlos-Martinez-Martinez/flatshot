# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in FlatShot a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics. We value respectful, specific, evidence-based collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing confidential or identifying data.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope while remaining free to disagree respectfully.
- Give credit and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or sustained bad-faith behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban participants. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation is prohibited.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
